Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from parents and students.

What are the batch timings for Bengali and English Medium tuition? ▼
Bengali Medium (WBBSE) tuition batches run in the morning from 6:30 AM – 9:30 AM, while English Medium (CBSE / CISCE) batches take place in the afternoon from 4:30 PM – 7:30 PM.
Do you offer Home Tuition support? ▼
Yes, we provide reliable and qualified home tutors for individual, one-on-one academic support around Jayantipur, Khalitpur, Petrapole, and neighboring areas.
How do I register for co-curricular activities like Abacus, Dance, or Yoga? ▼
You can select your desired course in the Admission Form below or click the WhatsApp button to inform us directly. We will provide class schedules and batch allocations.
Where are the United Students Academy centers located? ▼
Our primary learning centers operate at Jayantipur Bazar and Khalitpur, near Petrapole, West Bengal.
